Nokshi Store | Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

Turning Sales Data into Smarter Marketing Decisions with Microsoft Excel

📌 Project Overview

Where should a fashion retailer in Bangladesh spend its next advertising Taka?

This project explores that question through an interactive sales analytics dashboard built in Microsoft Excel for Nokshi Store, a fictional Bangladeshi fashion retailer.

Using a simulated dataset of approximately 30,000 orders from 2025, I analyzed sales performance, customer demographics, regional performance, and sales channels to identify opportunities for more targeted marketing and better business decisions.

This was my first-ever data analytics project, where I applied Excel to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

🎯 Business Problem

Retail businesses can spend money across multiple advertising platforms without clearly understanding which customer segments, regions, or sales channels deserve the most attention.

The objective of this project was to explore:

- Which customer segments contribute most to sales?
- Which divisions generate the highest sales?
- Which age groups and genders account for the most orders?
- Which sales channels contribute the largest share of orders?
- How do sales and order volumes change throughout the year?
- How can these findings inform future marketing budget allocation?

📊 Dashboard Features

The interactive dashboard includes:

- Monthly Sales Trends: Tracks monthly sales and order volumes throughout 2025.
- Regional Sales Analysis: Identifies the top five divisions by sales.
- Customer Gender Analysis: Compares sales contributions by gender.
- Age and Gender Segmentation: Examines order distribution across customer age groups and genders.
- Sales Channel Analysis: Compares order contributions from different purchasing channels.
- Order Status Analysis: Visualizes delivered, cancelled, returned, and refunded orders.
- Interactive Filters: Uses Excel slicers to explore results by month, product category, and sales channel.

🔍 Key Findings

Analysis of the simulated dataset produced the following findings:

Metric| Finding
Sales by gender| Women accounted for 72% of sales
Regional performance| Dhaka and Chattogram led sales, followed by Khulna
Customer age| Customers aged 30–49 accounted for 54% of orders
Sales channels| Facebook, Daraz, and the website accounted for 74% of orders

These findings highlight potential opportunities for customer segmentation, regional targeting, and channel-specific marketing.

💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the observed patterns, I recommend the following strategies for the fictional retailer:

1. Prioritize relevant customer segments: Test targeted campaigns aimed at women aged 30–49, while monitoring performance across other segments.
2. Focus on high-performing regions: Investigate opportunities to strengthen customer acquisition and retention in Dhaka, Chattogram, and Khulna.
3. Optimize channel investment: Evaluate Facebook advertising, Daraz promotions, and website campaigns based on their conversion rates, customer acquisition costs, and profitability.
4. Use data-driven promotions: Test website coupons and marketplace offers to determine whether they generate incremental sales rather than simply discounting purchases that would have happened anyway.
5. Monitor performance over time: Review monthly sales, order volumes, and order statuses to identify changes and evaluate marketing effectiveness.

These recommendations are hypotheses based on simulated data. Actual advertising budget allocation should be validated using real campaign costs, conversion data, margins, and customer behavior.
